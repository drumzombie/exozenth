Privacy and cookie choices

Last updated: November 17, 2026.

Who we are

Exo Zenth is a music project of Glenn Corcoran, who operates exozenth.com. For privacy questions, use our contact form or email privacy@exozenth.com.

The short version

  • Google Analytics and embedded YouTube and Spotify players stay off unless you click Accept.
  • Streaming links and the contact form work whether you accept or reject.
  • We do not sell your information for money. We do not install Meta Pixel or save campaign attribution records.
  • You can change your choice at any time using Cookie settings in the footer.

What we collect

On every visit. Cloudflare hosts and delivers this site. It processes technical information such as your IP address, browser, requested page and time to deliver and protect the site. Fonts and other site assets are served from this site.

Only after Accept. Google Analytics measures page visits and outbound link clicks. It may receive the page address, referral and campaign parameters in that address, browser and device information, and your IP address in processing the request. Our custom outbound event includes only the destination URL and current page path. We do not send contact email links or form contents as click events. Advertising storage and advertising consent signals stay denied.

Players. YouTube and Spotify players load only after Accept and may process technical and usage information under their own policies. YouTube uses privacy-enhanced embed URLs. The featured YouTube video starts automatically with the sound off after Accept, including on later visits while your consent is saved. Choosing a preset also starts it muted. You can unmute in the player. Before Accept, use the streaming links instead.

Contact requests. When you submit our contact form, your required email address, optional name, request type and message go to Formspree so we can receive and respond to your request. The contact page loads no third-party form scripts. Formspree may apply its own spam checks when you submit. Email sent directly goes through the email services involved. We keep correspondence as needed to handle your request and any applicable recordkeeping requirements. The site does not set an automatic deletion period for messages. See Formspree's privacy policy.

Your choices

Accept allows Google Analytics and embedded players. Reject keeps them off. Use Cookie settings at any time. We remember your choice for six months. With JavaScript disabled, optional services stay off and the public email link still works. The contact form uses a plain HTML submission.

If you switch from Accept to Reject, we remove accessible first-party Google and Meta tracking cookies and any legacy campaign or debug records, then reload to unload optional services. We cannot delete information already sent to providers or cookies and storage belonging to their sites. Use browser and provider controls for those.

Global Privacy Control (GPC). When your browser sends GPC, we treat it as Reject, keep players off and disable Accept. This signal can come from a browser privacy setting, a private or incognito window, a browser set to always use private browsing, or a privacy extension. To allow optional services, turn the signal off in your browser or extension and reload. Private browsing can also forget your saved choice when the private session ends. Streaming links remain available.

Sharing

Cloudflare processes information to host and protect the site. After Accept, Google and the player providers process information to run and measure their services. Formspree receives contact form submissions independently of your cookie choice. Providers may process information in other countries and retain it according to their policies and settings.

See, correct or delete your information

Use the contact form or email privacy@exozenth.com to ask to see, correct or delete your information, or to unsubscribe. Include the email address involved so we can locate it. We may need to verify that the request comes from you. For information held by Google or player providers, you may also need their privacy controls.

Children

This site is for a general audience and isn't directed at children. We don't knowingly collect children's information. If you think a child has sent us information, contact us and we'll remove it.

Other sites and changes

This page covers exozenth.com. External streaming, social and other linked sites have their own policies. We will update this page when our practices change and before adding new tracking tools.
